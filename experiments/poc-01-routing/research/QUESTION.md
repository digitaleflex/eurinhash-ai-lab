# Research Question

Can capability-aware routing reduce effective mission cost while preserving verified task success compared with strongest-first, cheapest-first, random, and established routing baselines?

## Hypothesis

IF model selection incorporates task capability requirements in addition to cost and quality signals, THEN verified success per effective cost can improve, BECAUSE model-task fit should reduce unnecessary use of stronger models and avoid underpowered dispatches.

This is a falsifiable hypothesis, not an established result.
